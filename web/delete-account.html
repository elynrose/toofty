Delete your Toofty account

Request removal of your account and associated data from Toofty.

What we delete

  • Your Firebase authentication account (email / Google sign-in)
  • Your user profile in our database
  • All children profiles you created
  • Brushing history, scores, and reward claims
  • Custom rewards and app settings stored for your account

What we may keep

  • Records we are required to retain for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes (if any), for the minimum period required by law
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics that cannot be linked back to you

How to request deletion

Email us from the address linked to your Toofty account. Include “Account deletion request” in the subject line. We will verify ownership and process your request within 30 days.

Email deletion request

Or send an email manually to briktapinc@gmail.com from your registered address.
