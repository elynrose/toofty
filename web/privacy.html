Toofty Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Toofty ("we", "our") is a family brushing helper app operated by Briktap Inc. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

  • Account information: email address and authentication identifiers when you sign in (email/password or Google).
  • Profile data: children’s names, dates of birth, monster preferences, brushing history, points, and rewards you configure.
  • Photos (optional): images you choose to attach to rewards, stored on your device.

How we use data

  • Provide and sync your account across devices
  • Track brushing progress and reward points
  • Authenticate users and secure parent-only settings

Where data is stored

Account data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) in the United States. Brushing and child profile data is stored locally on your device and associated with your account.

Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and cloud database
  • Google Sign-In — optional login method

Children

Toofty is designed for use by parents managing children’s brushing routines. Parents create child profiles; we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental involvement.

Data retention & deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time: Account deletion request or email hello@toofty.com.
