Support

We are here to help your family get the most out of Toofty.

Contact us

For questions about accounts, brushing sessions, rewards, billing, or technical issues, email our support team. We typically respond within 2 business days.

Email support

Direct email: briktapinc@gmail.com

Common topics

Sign-in help

Use the same email or Google account you registered with. Check spam folders for verification emails.

Parent PIN

Settings and point adjustments are protected by your parent PIN. Reset it from the app if you forget it.

Rewards and points

Parents customize rewards in Settings. Kids earn points for completed brushing sessions, up to twice per day.

Sync across devices

Sign in with the same account on each device. Data syncs through your Firebase account.

Account deletion

To request removal of your account and associated data, visit our account deletion page or email us with Account deletion request in the subject line from your registered address.
