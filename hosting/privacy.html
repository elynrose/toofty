Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Toofty ("we", "our", "us") is a family brushing helper app operated by Briktap Inc. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Toofty, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and authentication identifiers when you sign in with email/password or Google Sign-In.
  • Profile data: children's names, dates of birth, monster preferences, brushing history, points, rewards, and settings you configure as a parent.
  • Photos (optional): images you choose to attach to rewards, stored on your device and associated with your account data.
  • Usage data: app interactions such as brushing sessions completed, points earned, and reward claims, linked to your account.

How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Toofty app
  • Authenticate users and secure parent-only settings
  • Sync your account and child profiles across devices
  • Track brushing progress and reward points
  • Respond to support requests and legal obligations

Where data is stored

Account and profile data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) in the United States. Brushing and child profile data may also be stored locally on your device and associated with your account for offline use and sync.

Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google): authentication, cloud database, and hosting
  • Google Sign-In: optional login method

These providers process data according to their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Children and parental control

Toofty is designed for use by parents and guardians managing children's brushing routines. Parents create and manage child profiles. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental involvement through a parent-owned account.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. See our account deletion page for instructions.

Your rights and choices

  • Access or update account information in the app settings
  • Request account deletion via email or our deletion page
  • Contact us with privacy questions at hello@toofty.com

Security

We use industry-standard measures through Firebase and secure authentication to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use a strong password and keep your parent PIN private.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made. Continued use of Toofty after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
