--- PROMOTIONAL TEXT (170 chars, optional) ---
Help kids build brushing habits with guided timers, cute monster buddies, and parent-set rewards.

--- DESCRIPTION ---
Toofty turns tooth brushing into something kids actually look forward to. Friendly monster buddies guide children through step-by-step brushing sessions while parents track progress and set meaningful rewards.

FOR KIDS
• Follow a fun, guided brushing routine with on-screen timers
• Watch your monster buddy show you where and how to brush
• Earn points every time you complete a session (up to twice a day)
• Celebrate when you're done!
• Pick a monster friend and enjoy music during brushing

FOR PARENTS
• Create profiles for each child in your family
• Customize brushing steps, durations, and instructions
• Set up rewards kids can work toward — ice cream trips, toy shopping, extra screen time, and more
• Edit, add, or remove rewards anytime
• Track brushing history on a calendar
• Protect settings with a parent PIN
• Sign in with email or Google to sync across devices

HOW IT WORKS
1. Add your child and choose their monster buddy
2. Start a brushing session — Toofty walks them through each zone
3. Kids earn 10 points per completed session
4. When they have enough points, they can claim rewards you've set up

Toofty is designed for families who want a simple, positive way to build healthy brushing habits — without nagging.
